Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026 • Version 1.0

YourDay is an open-source daily companion app built with Kotlin Multiplatform that brings together your weather forecast, scheduled calendar events, and task list into a unified overview. We believe your personal schedule and daily routines belong solely to you.

1. Overview & Guiding Philosophy

YourDay is designed from the ground up around local-first data processing and user privacy. The application does not require you to create a proprietary account with us, does not sell your personal data, and does not run any third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.

All sensitive personal records (such as your calendar events, task lists, and preference settings) remain on your local device or are transmitted directly between your device and the third-party APIs you choose to connect.

2. Information Collection and Use

A. Location Information (Weather)

To provide current local weather conditions and hourly forecasts, YourDay requests device location permission (approximate or precise).

  • Purpose: Your coordinates (latitude and longitude) are sent directly to WeatherAPI (api.weatherapi.com) to retrieve localized weather data.
  • Storage: Your coordinates are neither saved on our servers nor combined with any personal identifiers or location histories. If you prefer, you can search for a city name instead of granting GPS access.

B. Calendar Events

YourDay requests read-only calendar access to show your schedule alongside your tasks.

  • Android: Uses the system CalendarContract content provider.
  • iOS: Uses Apple's native EventKit framework.
  • Local-only execution: Your calendar entries (event titles, times, attendees, locations) are read entirely on your device in memory. They are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party service.

C. Task Management (Todoist / TickTick)

If you choose to link your Todoist or TickTick account, YourDay enables you to view and interact with your tasks:

  • OAuth Authorization: You authenticate directly with the provider via a standard OAuth flow. Your password is never seen or collected by YourDay.
  • Token Storage: The resulting OAuth access token is saved locally on your device in encrypted local storage (Jetpack DataStore preferences).
  • Direct Communication: Task queries and updates communicate directly between your device and the provider's API endpoints (e.g., api.todoist.com). No intermediate proxy server is used.
  • Revocation: You can disconnect your task accounts at any time inside the app settings, which immediately purges the token from your device.

D. User Preferences & Local Storage

Your selected settings (temperature unit °C/°F, selected city, task filter preferences, and theme choices) are stored strictly locally on your device using DataStore.

E. Diagnostics & Crash Reporting

To help identify unexpected errors and ensure stability across different devices and OS versions, YourDay uses Firebase Crashlytics.

  • Data Collected: Anonymous device state information, OS version, device model, and stack traces when a crash occurs.
  • No PII: Crash logs do not include personal user data, calendar contents, or task names.

3. Device Permissions Used

The app requests the following system permissions to provide core features:

  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION (Android) & NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription (iOS): Used solely to fetch weather for your immediate location.
  • READ_CALENDAR (Android) & NSCalendarsUsageDescription (iOS): Used solely to display your scheduled events on your daily agenda.
  • INTERNET: Required to fetch weather forecasts and synchronize tasks with your connected task management accounts.
You can grant, modify, or revoke any of these permissions at any time through your operating system's device settings.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

YourDay interacts with select external APIs to deliver weather forecasts, task synchronization, and stability diagnostics. Each service governs data according to its own privacy policy:

  • WeatherAPI: Provides weather forecasts.
    WeatherAPI Privacy Policy
  • Todoist / Doist: Provides task integration when enabled by the user.
    Todoist Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymous crash diagnostic logs.
    Firebase Privacy Information

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because YourDay does not maintain user accounts or central databases:

  • All local preferences, cached weather snippets, and OAuth tokens are permanently removed when you clear the app storage or uninstall YourDay from your device.
  • You can disconnect third-party integrations at any moment from the Settings screen.
  • You may revoke OAuth authorizations at any time directly through your Todoist or third-party account management settings.

6. Security

We are committed to protecting your information. All network communications with external providers (WeatherAPI, Todoist, Firebase) are strictly transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Sensitive tokens are kept in private app storage protected by operating system sandboxing.

7. Children's Privacy

YourDay does not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the application, please contact us and we will take immediate steps to address it.
